# Grasp — Project Instructions

## Decision logging (standing instruction)

Whenever you make a non-trivial implementation decision that isn't explicitly pinned down by `grasp-project-brief.md` — a library choice, a threshold, a naming or structural tradeoff, or anything else with more than one reasonable answer — append an entry to `DECISIONS.md` documenting the decision and your reasoning **before moving on to the next piece of work**. Use the template already at the top of that file. Do this proactively, without waiting to be asked.

This does not apply to decisions the brief already settles (e.g. stack, gating default, question design) — only to gaps you're filling in yourself.
